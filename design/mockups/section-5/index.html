Section 5: four options

All live. Whichever isn’t chosen can become section 6 or 7 — the page still has room for two more.

1 · Nobody else joins the call

Trust. The ringed-meadow film, three people taking their seats, and a dashed fourth seat that draws itself: “No bot here”. Three quiet facts underneath.

2 · Your room, your light

Make it yours. One big camera tile that changes room every four seconds through the app’s real backgrounds, each with its own camera style, while the accent steps through the five colours.

3 · “Let’s pick this up Thursday.” Booked.

The follow-up. The call plays on the right and the app’s own proposal card rises over it with the time it heard, the clash check and the invitees.

4 · You talked. It wrote everything down.

The notes. The summary and decisions, action items ticking off one by one, with the recording, the sharing warning and the mind map beside them.
