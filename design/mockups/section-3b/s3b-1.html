Everything else
you’d expect, done properly.

The small things that make a call feel easy, each one as considered as the big ones.

  1. Captions, in the languages you mix

    Hindi and English in one sentence? Morse follows along.

  2. 18 languages

    Ten understood without a setting, eight more when you ask.

  3. A whiteboard in the call

    Sketch it out together, everyone’s cursor on the board.

  4. Draw on what’s shared

    Circle the number that matters while you present.

  5. Reactions and raised hands

    Pixel emoji float up the stage; hands wait their turn.

  6. Notes, drawn as a map

    Every meeting’s notes branch out, section by section.
