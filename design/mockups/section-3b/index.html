Section 3: everything else, four layouts

These are live: every feature is animating. Scroll inside the frames for layouts 3 and 4.

1 · Index and stage

The six features as a large-type index on the left; one big stage on the right plays the chosen feature. It moves to the next every seven seconds, or on hover.

2 · Constellation

Six pieces scattered at different sizes on open canvas, all playing at once, captions under each.

3 · Filmstrip

Tall cards in one row with wide gaps; drag or scroll sideways. Each plays when it comes into view.

4 · Stacked cards

One feature per card, text beside the animation. Cards stick and stack on top of one another as you scroll.
