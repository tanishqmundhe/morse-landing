Section 4: four stories, all animated

These are live. Each is a story the page hasn't told yet; whichever isn't chosen here can become section 5 or 6.

1 · Nobody else joins the call (trust)

The ringed-meadow film plays behind; the three people slide into their seats one by one; the fourth seat's dashed outline draws itself and reads “No bot here”; Recording breathes. Three quiet lines underneath.

2 · A page to book you (booking)

A 12-second loop of the real flow: Thursday gets picked, the times rise, 2:00 is chosen, the name types itself, “Booked”, then the view slides to Priya’s week and the call drops into Thursday. The three steps on the left light up in time.

3 · Your room, your light (make it yours)

A big camera tile that changes room every three seconds through the app’s real backgrounds, each with a different camera style, while the accent ring steps through the five colours and repaints the New meeting button.

4 · Five apps, or one (why switch)

A handle sweeps across the same meeting: the grey five-app stack with a notetaker bot on one side, Morse on the other. It can be dragged; it sweeps on its own until you do.
