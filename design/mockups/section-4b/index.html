Section 4: four framings

The two cards in the middle are the real ones, unchanged. Only the words and the layout around them differ.

1 · Centred

Title, one line, the shareable link, then the cards. The three points sit quietly underneath.

2 · Copy left, cards right

Everything to read on the left, the page itself on the right. Nothing sits above the cards.

3 · The link as the page’s address bar

The link becomes a bar attached to the top of the cards, so they read as one page you can share. Points in a row below.

4 · Big type, cards bleeding off

Oversized headline on the left; the cards run off the right edge of the screen.
