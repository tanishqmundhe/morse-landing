A · Film window Chosen, built on the site

The signal film fills a large rounded window with everything laid over it. As built: the code row above the headline is gone, and the corner card now plays one meeting through Notes, Teleprompter and Follow-up.

B · Split stage

Closest to the Forgeflow reference: framed edges, copy left, the ringed-meadow film right with the Teleprompter answer writing itself in and the call controls under it. A slow MORSE code strip divides it from a statement paragraph that lights up as you scroll.

C · Rooms

A centred headline over a row of meeting tiles, each with a film as its background, the way the app’s backgrounds work. The speaking highlight moves between tiles and a caption appears on the speaker. Floating pill nav.

D · Signal

The name spelled out in the logo’s own dots and dashes. A coral pulse travels along it before the headline arrives; the gold tunnel film sits dim behind; the app’s today card rises from below as you scroll.
